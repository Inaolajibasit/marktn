## Scope and method

All 13 pages were extracted as text, rendered, and visually reviewed. This document records the substantive content, image-only wording, service hierarchy, and relevant presentation observations. Page references use the PDF's physical page order. Repeated navigation lists and NEXT labels are consolidated rather than repeated on every page. Line breaks, spacing, and broken extraction characters are normalized for readability. Source wording that may need editorial clarification is identified separately. Statements describe what the supplied deck says; company claims have not been independently verified. No website copy or configuration was changed as part of this extraction.

2. Page 3: PDF extraction includes an isolated “(MA” fragment that is not meaningful visible body copy. It is excluded from the company description.
3. Page 6: the text layer contains broken spacing and stray characters in the supporting phrases. The rendered page clearly reads Campaigns rooted in cultural truth, Immersive brand experiences, Long-term narratives, and Africa as a primary market.
4. Page 7: the text layer corrupts Africa's; the rendered page confirms the intended possessive used above.
5. Page 8: retain Excellent as the value name unless the client approves a wording change. The Fresh & New description has the grammar issue recorded in section 7.
6. Page 9: Influencer & Community Market is the visible heading; confirm whether Marketing was intended.
7. Page 9: a Campaign Production heading is partly clipped at the bottom. Page 10 provides the complete heading and its capabilities.
